❓ How to Play

  1. Yipee the Lamb gives you a Holy Bible verse scrambled into word bubbles on the hill — plus a few trick words!
  2. Tap the words in the correct order, one at a time. Your pet jumps for joy on every correct word!
  3. Yipee the Lamb pops out to give scripture cheers on streaks and milestones!
  4. Each correct tap flies onto Yipee's Bible verse board until the verse is complete.
  5. 💡 Peek gives you 3 hints to highlight the next right word on the hill!
  6. Game Difficulties:
    🌟 Easy (60s): 50% of the verse is already filled in! Tap the remaining half (1 trick word).
    🌟🌟 Medium (80s): Phrase-by-phrase! Each phrase has its initial word revealed (1 trick word).
    🌟🌟🌟 Hard (100s): Full verse from memory! 0 words revealed, 2+ trick words!
